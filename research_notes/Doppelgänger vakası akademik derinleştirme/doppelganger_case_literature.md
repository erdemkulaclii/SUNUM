## Q1. Peer-reviewed and academic work on Doppelgänger / SDA / Structura (with DOIs)

### Takeaway
There is very little peer-reviewed scholarship devoted to Doppelgänger itself. Most rigorous knowledge sits in government, platform and NGO reports. The one peer-reviewed article located that is explicitly about Doppelgänger is a 2024 Polish law/security journal piece. The most substantial academic-institutional study is the Lund University PDRI report by Pamment & Tsurtsumia (2025), which is not peer-reviewed in the journal sense. arXiv has only adjacent work. For an academic presentation, this scarcity is itself worth saying: the evidence base is mostly grey literature.

### Cited Findings

**A. Peer-reviewed (journal) — Doppelgänger-specific**
- Gergelewicz, T. (2024). "Fake Mirroring in Cyberspace as a Disinformation Tool – Doppelgangers and Deepfakes." *Cybersecurity and Law*, 2024(1). DOI listed as **10.35467/cal/188396** **[VERIFY: DOI shown on the Library of Science index page but not resolved; issue/page numbers not confirmed]**. ## Q2. What the key primary and grey sources establish (date, numbers, attribution, targets)

### Takeaway
The record runs in a clear sequence. The NGO exposé (EU DisinfoLab/Qurium) and the Meta takedown came on the same day, 27 Sep 2022. Meta attributed the operation to SDA and Structura in its Q4 2022 report. France (June 2023) and the EU (July 2023) followed, then Germany (June 2024), US Treasury (March 2024) and the DOJ affidavit and domain seizures (Sept 2024). Leak-based analyses followed in 2024–2025. The headline numbers differ a lot depending on the unit counted: brands, websites, domains or accounts.

### Cited Findings

**1. EU DisinfoLab with Qurium — "Doppelganger: Media clones serving Russian propaganda" (27 Sep 2022)**
- Active in Europe since at least May 2022. Cloned at least 17 media brands (e.g., Bild, 20minutes, Ansa, The Guardian, RBC Ukraine) to publish fake articles, videos and polls — [EU DisinfoLab PDF](https://www.disinfo.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Doppelganger-1.pdf); [landing page](https://www.disinfo.eu/doppelganger/); [NSArchive mirror dated 2022-09-27](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/semon9-giki0/2022-09-27-EUDisinfoLab-Qurium-Doppelganger.pdf)
- Tactics: spoofed domain names, videos falsely attributed to real media, smart redirections and geo-blocking. Amplified by Facebook Pages and fake profiles that were mostly abandoned after use ("burner" accounts). Qurium found Keitaro software (company registered in Estonia) enabling the redirects — [EU DisinfoLab PDF](https://www.disinfo.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Doppelganger-1.pdf)
- Attribution in 2022: the operators were **not identified**, and a false-flag operation could not be fully ruled out — [EU DisinfoLab PDF](https://www.disinfo.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Doppelganger-1.pdf)
- Later work: EU DisinfoLab keeps a running "Doppelganger hub" — [hub](https://www.disinfo.eu/doppelganger-hub/). Qurium's follow-ups cover the hosting supply chain, e.g., how AEZA's infrastructure survived sanctions — [Qurium AEZA](https://www.qurium.org/doppelganger/sanctioned-still-connected-how-aezas-infrastructure-supply-chain-survived/); [Qurium "evil empire"](https://www.qurium.org/alerts/exposing-the-evil-empire-of-doppelganger-disinformation/)

**3. France, VIGINUM/SGDSN — "RRN: une campagne numérique de manipulation de l'information complexe et persistante" (13 June 2023, updated 19 June 2023)**
- Campaign active against several European states including France **since September 2022**, aimed at undermining Western support for Ukraine. ### Inferences
- **The counts are not comparable, and the presentation must say so.** EU DisinfoLab reports **17 media brands**. Meta and Treasury report **"60+ websites"**. VIGINUM reports **355 domains** by June 2023. The user's Meta CSV has **311 unique domains**. DOJ seized **32 domains** in 2024. These count different units (brand, website, domain, indicator) at different dates. The user's 311 is closer in scale to VIGINUM's 355 than to the "60 websites" figure. ## Q3. Evolution after 2022 and what it means for reading a 2022 indicator snapshot

### Takeaway
Doppelgänger changed a lot after its 2022 debut. Its targets widened from mainly Germany to France (2023), the US (2023–2024), Israel and others. Its distribution moved from Facebook Pages and ads to large X bot networks and paid verified accounts, and to Meta ads despite sanctions. It added AI-generated content, obfuscated redirect chains and Keitaro cloaking, and it rebuilt domains fast after takedowns. A 2022 indicator file is therefore a snapshot of the first phase, shaped by Meta's own detection and labelling, not a picture of the whole campaign.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 phase: spoofed domains, redirects and geo-blocking via Keitaro, burner Facebook Pages — [EU DisinfoLab 2022](https://www.disinfo.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Doppelganger-1.pdf). Meta targets in 2022: "primarily Germany", plus France, Italy, Ukraine and the UK — [Meta Q3 2022](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/11/metas-adversarial-threat-report-q3-2022/)
- Exploiting gaps in domain registration (cybersquatting/typosquatting) — [Meta Q4 2022](https://about.fb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Meta-Quarterly-Adversarial-Threat-Report-Q4-2022.pdf)
- 2023: France via RRN and spoofing of the French MFA website; 355 domains — [SGDSN](https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/publications/maj-19062023-rrn-une-campagne-numerique-de-manipulation-de-linformation-complexe-et).
